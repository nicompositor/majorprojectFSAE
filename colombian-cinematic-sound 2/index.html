01 / THE PROJECTA SOUND IN THE MAKING

Rooted in a place.
Open to the world.

What happens when the sounds of a territory begin to tell stories beyond its borders?

This project explores a contemporary Colombian identity for film music. Gaita, tiple, marimba de chonta and percussion meet orchestral writing, electronics and sound design—not as geographical shorthand, but as expressive voices.

Beginning with the memory of displacement in Alfredo Molano’s Desterrados: crónicas del desarraigo, the research moves from literature to an original suite, and from that sonic language to moving images.

04 / RESEARCH & PROCESSLISTEN. EXPERIMENT. REFLECT.

Composition as
a way of asking.

I

Listen to the context

Literature, historical memory, Colombian musical practices and film references establish the questions behind the work. Cultural identity is approached as a lived relationship, not a fixed collection of sounds.

II

Transform the material

Improvisation, extended techniques, sampling and processing reveal new possibilities in familiar instruments. Breath, resonance, attacks and rhythmic fragments become compositional material.

III

Write for the story

Orchestration, mockups, recording and synchronisation test the palette in different settings. Reflection concerns both the dramatic effect of a musical decision and its relationship to its source.

A NOTE ON CREATIVE ETHICS

These works offer a situated interpretation—not a single definition of Colombian identity, and not a claim to speak on behalf of displaced communities.
